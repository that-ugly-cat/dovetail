What the columns mean

An unknown fee is not a free journal. The fee is missing for about nine journals in ten, so a consultation never excludes a journal for it: it marks it for checking.

Subjects unknown means nothing is on record about what the journal publishes. It cannot be compared with a manuscript, which is different from being a poor match.

Repositories and book series are kept so that you can see what a consultation brought back, and are never proposed: a repository is where a paper is deposited, not where it is submitted.
