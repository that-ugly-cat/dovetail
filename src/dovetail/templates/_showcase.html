Dovetail

Where to send a paper. From a title, an abstract and, if you have it, the reference list, Dovetail lists candidate journals with the reasons for each — and says plainly what it could not check.

A list, not a ranking

Each journal is compared with your manuscript, among the journals found that day. A place in the list says nothing about the journal in general, and nothing here pretends it does.

Merit and logistics apart

What makes a journal a good home for the argument is kept separate from cost, access and speed. A journal that does well only on the second kind is marked.

Unknown is not excluded

A journal is never removed because a fact about it is missing or out of date. It stays in the list, marked for you to check.
