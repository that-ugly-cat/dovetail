Dovetail

Where to send a paper. Given a title, an abstract and a word count, it produces a list of candidate journals — with the criteria that hold each one up, sorted into merit and logistics, and with the ones it cannot vouch for marked as such.

A list, not a ranking

The score is computed against one paper, over whatever set the sweep reached that day. A position means nothing outside its own run, so nothing here pretends otherwise.

Merit and logistics apart

A journal held up only by open-access status and turnaround is marked, however well it does on those. Breadth of audience is not fit of argument.

Missing is not excluded

No constraint ever removes a journal on a field that is absent or out of date. It stays, marked for checking, and goes to the front of the queue.
