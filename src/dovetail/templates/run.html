A journal standing on fewer than two criteria of merit is marked, however well it does on logistics. That rule comes from a real choice that rested on three logistical reasons and one about merit — and it was the one about merit that failed.

The score measures how concentrated a journal is on this subject, not how suitable it is. The cosine is invariant to scale but not to shape, so against a narrow manuscript it rewards narrow journals. Those are different things, and the score itself is not yet validated.
