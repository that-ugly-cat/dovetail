The manuscript

At least {{ min_words }} words: a shorter text is classified too unreliably for the list to mean anything, and Dovetail will decline. Beyond about 300 words the end is left out, and the consultation says so.

With it, Dovetail also finds the journals whose recent papers cite the works you cite. That reaches journals the abstract alone does not: a paper about a technology or a disease is otherwise matched to the journals of that technology or disease. References without a DOI — books, mostly — are not read.

Constraints — optional

The word count is checked against each journal's limits, where they are on record. A funder excludes the journals it does not pay for. A journal whose fee or access is unknown is never removed: it is marked for you to check.

A journal is kept when at least a fifth of its articles are in that language, so bilingual journals stay. The language is read from the journal's own articles the first time a consultation meets it, which is the last line of the cost below; a journal whose language could not be found is marked, not removed.

The Leiden core list

A list of journals kept by CWTS, the centre at Leiden University that publishes the Leiden Ranking. It is the set of journals that ranking counts, and OpenAlex says of each journal whether it is on it. Of the {{ '{:,}'.format(core_counts.all) }} journals Dovetail knows, {{ '{:,}'.format(core_counts.on) }} are.

Left unticked

Every journal is considered. The list you get is wider: beside the journals everybody knows it shows national and society journals, small publishers, and now and then a title that has stopped publishing or a record that is not really a journal. Choosing a language removes most of the journals you could not submit to anyway.

Ticked

Only journals on the list are proposed. The result is shorter and has fewer surprises. It also leaves out journals you may want: nearly everything not published in English, many national journals, and some well-known titles — Health Affairs and Swiss Medical Weekly are not on it.

Being on the list means the ranking counts the journal. It is not a mark of quality, and being off it is not a warning. The list was computed from data of August 2024, so a journal founded since is not on it. Source: CWTS, «Core sources and core publications in OpenAlex», read on 20 Nov 2026.
