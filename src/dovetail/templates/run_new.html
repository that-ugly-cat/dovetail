The manuscript

At least {{ min_words }} words: a shorter text is classified too unreliably for the list to mean anything, and Dovetail will decline. Beyond about 300 words the end is left out, and the consultation says so.

With it, Dovetail also finds the journals whose recent papers cite the works you cite. That reaches journals the abstract alone does not: a paper about a technology or a disease is otherwise matched to the journals of that technology or disease. References without a DOI — books, mostly — are not read.

Constraints — optional

The word count is checked against each journal's limits, where they are on record. A funder excludes the journals it does not pay for. A journal whose fee or access is unknown is never removed: it is marked for you to check.
