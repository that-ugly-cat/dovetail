Unknown is a third answer, and it survives the form. Leaving «in DOAJ» or «open access» unknown is not the same as saying no: a constraint never excludes on a missing field, it marks the journal for checking. Ticking a box you have not verified would turn «nobody looked» into «no», which is the one substitution the whole tool is built to refuse.

A declared journal arrives unclassifiable and stays that way until it has a profile: it cannot be scored, and its zero means «I don't know». The profile is built from articles it actually published — dovetail profile-venue — which costs 100 credits per article, so five to ten from different years is the right size. Ten from one special issue describe that issue, not the journal.
