What these numbers mean

A consultation costs OpenAlex credits: 100 to classify the manuscript, a few dozen to search for journals, and one per reference when you paste a reference list. «Consultations left» counts only the first of those.

Journals grow with use. Dovetail keeps every journal a consultation has looked up. It is not a census: a journal missing here has simply not come up yet.

A journal with no known subjects is not a bad match. Nothing is on record about what it publishes, so it cannot be compared at all. In a consultation it is listed apart, under «could not be assessed».
